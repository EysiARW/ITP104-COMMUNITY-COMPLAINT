Technical Architecture & Capstone Defense Guide: Data Analytics

1. Executive Overview & Strategic Purpose

In the Barangay Banlic CCIRS, Data Analytics transforms isolated, transactional complaints into actionable governance intelligence. Rather than treating resident feedback merely as ad-hoc tickets, the system aggregates operational data to empower barangay executives and committee chairpersons to transition from reactive problem solving to proactive, evidence-based governance.

2. Academic & Operational Analytics Framework

The system implements the standard three-tier data analytics framework:

Analytics Tier Core Question Addressed Implementation in Banlic-CCIRS
1. Descriptive Analytics What is happening in the barangay right now? Real-time counters for submissions, in-progress cases, and resolution rates. Categorical distribution across noise, sanitation, and peace & order.
2. Diagnostic Analytics Where are the delays and localized incident concentrations? Geospatial Purok Bar Charts identifying community hotspots. Automated >48h Overdue SLA indicators flagging triage bottlenecks.
3. Prescriptive / Trend Insights What preventive actions should the council take? 6-Month historical rolling trendlines detecting seasonal spikes, guiding proactive Tanod patrols and localized municipal budget allocations.
Key Operational Value: When sanitation or road obstruction complaints surge in a specific sitio (e.g. Purok 4), the barangay council uses the analytics engine to immediately deploy dedicated waste collection rounds and tanod monitoring, resolving root causes before issues escalate.

3. Data Visualization Architecture

The visualization suite is built on Chart.js with bidirectional reactive theme synchronization (supporting both Light and Dark modes):

  • 6-Month Report Volume Trend (Smooth Line Chart): Plots monthly submission trajectory across a rolling window to detect macro patterns.
  • Resolution Status Lifecycle (Doughnut Chart): Color-coded breakdown across all stages (Pending Review, Under Review, Assigned, In Progress, Resolved, Closed, Rejected).
  • Geographical Density by Purok / Area (Bar Chart): Quantitative comparison of incident frequency per sitio to guide on-the-ground patrol schedules.
  • Categorical Distribution (Bar Chart): Categorizes incidents by administrative committee (Peace & Order, Infrastructure, Sanitation, Women & Children).

4. Multi-Dimensional Querying & Data Interoperability

To support rigorous auditing, academic research, and municipal reporting, the analytics module includes:

  • Dynamic Multi-Factor Query Engine: Enables administrators to filter records concurrently by date range (date_from, date_to), category, status, priority, and purok with immediate dynamic metric re-calculation.
  • Automated CSV Export: Features UTF-8 BOM encoding for direct, zero-configuration compatibility with Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, PowerBI, and Tableau.
  • Executive Print & PDF Summary: Generates formal printable audit sheets suitable for monthly Barangay Council sessions and DILG compliance reports.

6. Anticipated Panel Questions & Answers (Q&A Defense Guide)

Q1: How does this qualify as Data Analytics rather than just a database report?

A: Simple reporting merely lists past events. Banlic-CCIRS applies analytical computation—including multi-dimensional cross-tabulations, rolling 6-month trend lines, geographic hotspot detection, and automated threshold alerts (>48h SLA warnings) that diagnose bottlenecks and prescribe targeted operational deployments.

Q2: How does the analytics engine protect citizen privacy under the Data Privacy Act?

A: All public-facing analytics and reference tracking utilize anonymized aggregations. Complainant names are cryptographically masked (e.g., J*** D***), and sensitive evidentiary files are stored in secured local disks accessible strictly to authenticated case handlers.

Q3: Can this data be utilized by external local government agencies like the Cabuyao City LGU?

A: Yes. The system features a native CSV streaming engine with UTF-8 encoding that outputs complete structured datasets, allowing seamless ingestion into municipal GIS, DILG reporting frameworks, and external business intelligence tools.
